Protect yourself in ten minutes

Four steps cover most of what keeps everyday accounts and devices safe. Work through them in order.

  1. Use a different password for every account

    A password manager remembers them for you. Protect it with a long master password made from three random words.

  2. Turn on two-factor authentication

    Start with your email and banking. An authenticator app is safer than text messages where you have the choice.

  3. Install updates when they appear

    Switch on automatic updates for your phone, computer and browser. Updates fix the weaknesses attackers rely on.

  4. Back up what matters

    Copy photos and documents to an external drive or a cloud service. Keep one copy that is not always connected.

If something goes wrong

  • Change the password for the affected account straight away.
  • Contact your bank first if money or card details are involved.
  • Report scams to Action Fraud.
  • Find more guidance from the National Cyber Security Centre.
